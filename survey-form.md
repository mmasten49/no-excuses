## Form title
Do you know what your habits cost you?

## Form description
Copy this exactly, including the line breaks:

> **THE PROBLEM**
> A lot of students spend money on one or two habits every week, like food runs, snacks or cash purchases. Each purchase feels small, so nobody adds them up. Tracking happens in your head, there is no weekly limit, and by the end of the month you have no idea what you spent. For one student that comes to somewhere between $260 and $600 a month, and they can't say which end of that range is true.
>
> **THE IDEA**
> A simple phone app for one or two habits, not your whole budget:
> - Log a purchase in a few seconds, right at the counter.
> - Month 1: track everything to learn your real starting number.
> - After that: a weekly limit that drops by 10% of your starting number each month, until you're spending half.
> - See at a glance how much of this week's limit is left.
> - "Trips left this week": instead of just "$18 left," it tells you "1 pizza trip left this week" based on what you usually spend per trip.
>
> Seven quick questions, about 2 minutes. Answers are anonymous. Please respond by Thursday, November 12.
