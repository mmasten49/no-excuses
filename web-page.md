# Web page

**App name:** No Excuses

## Headline
Cut your habit spending in half. No excuses.

## Subhead
Log every pizza run and cash purchase in seconds. Your weekly limit drops every four weeks, hit or miss, until you're spending half of what you started with.

## The problem, in the user's words
"Honestly, I have no clue what I spend on pizza. I go two to four times a week, it's like twenty bucks each time, and it never feels like a lot, so I just don't add it up. Same with cash. I cut back once because the people close to me got on me about it, but nobody's doing that for pizza. I'm guessing it's somewhere between $260 and $600 a month, and I genuinely can't tell you which."

## How the app helps
1. **You see the real number.** Spend the first four weeks just logging, so you learn what you actually spend instead of guessing.
2. **You see how many trips you have left before you buy.** The app turns your weekly limit into "1 pizza trip left," so you feel the limit before you spend, not after.
3. **The limit drops whether you're ready or not.** Every four weeks it falls by 10% of your starting number, until you're at half. Missing a target doesn't slow it down.

## What to do next
Open the app on your phone and log your last pizza run. It should take under 30 seconds.

## Look and feel
- **Tone:** direct and a little tough, like a coach, not a bank. Short sentences. No finance jargon and no exclamation points.
- **Colors:** an off-white background (#FAF7F2), near-black text (#1A1A1A), and one accent color: tomato red (#D7392B) for buttons, the "trips left" number and anything over the limit.
- **Font:** Inter. The headline is big and bold.
- **Layout:** one page, single column, designed for a phone first. The headline and subhead are at the top, the problem quote is set off as a pull quote, the three points are stacked, and there's one big red button for "What to do next."
- **Image:** a simple phone mockup of the Home screen showing the Pizza card with "1 pizza trip left" in big red text. No stock photos of people or money, and no pizza clip art.
