Questions

Straight answers.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You type each buy in yourself, which takes a few seconds. That's on purpose: no bank login, no account, and cash buys count just like card buys.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone, in your browser. Nothing goes to a server. The app confirms every save, and you can download a backup file anytime to protect your history or move it to a new phone.

What happens if I go over my limit?

The app shows you, and the card turns red. At the end of the block, the limit still drops to the next level. No repeats, no second chances. That's the "no excuses" part.

What about holidays and breaks?

You get one skip per four-week block. A skipped week doesn't count toward anything, and the block runs one week longer so your numbers stay honest.

Does it work without signal?

Yes, once you've opened it once with a connection. After that it loads from your phone, and everything you log saves right on the device.

What if I log the wrong amount?

Tap Undo in the bar that pops up after you save. Later on, open History and delete it with the trash button, then log it again.

Does it cost anything?

No. It's free, with no ads, no sign-up and no paid tier.

Do I need to download it from an app store?

No. It runs in your phone's browser. Open it, then tap Share → Add to Home Screen and it sits on your phone like any other app.
