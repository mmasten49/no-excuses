The problem

"Honestly, I have no clue what I spend on pizza. I go two to four times a week, it's like twenty bucks each time, and it never feels like a lot, so I just don't add it up. Same with cash. I cut back once because the people close to me got on me about it, but nobody's doing that for pizza. I'm guessing it's somewhere between $260 and $600 a month, and I genuinely can't tell you which."

How it helps

  1. You see the real number.Spend the first four weeks just logging, so you learn what you actually spend instead of guessing.
  2. You see how many trips you have left before you buy.The app turns your weekly limit into "1 pizza trip left," so you feel the limit before you spend, not after.
  3. The limit drops whether you're ready or not.Every four weeks it falls by 10% of your starting number, until you're at half. Missing a target doesn't slow it down.
