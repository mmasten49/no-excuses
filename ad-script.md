# Ad script

## Length
30 seconds.

## The scenes
| Seconds | On screen | Voice or text | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | **The hook.** A dark-red receipt prints down from the top of the frame on an off-white background, one line at a time: "Pizza $18", "Pizza $22", "Cash $40", "Pizza $15"… It keeps printing faster until it runs off the bottom of the frame. | Text: **"Where did $400 go?"** | The beat starts. Each receipt line lands with a soft tick, on the beat. |
| 4–10 | **The problem moment.** The receipt crumples into a ball and drops into the outline of a head. Inside the head, numbers spin like a slot machine: "$260? $450? $600?" They never stop on one. | Text: **"You're guessing."** then **"Every week."** | The ticks get louder. On the last spin, a record-scratch stop. |
| 10–20 | **The app solving it.** The head shape morphs into a phone showing the app's Home screen with two cards, Pizza and Cash. A cursor taps **+ Pizza**, types **$19** and taps **Save**. The big red number on the Pizza card drops from **"2 trips left"** to **"1 trip left."** | Text: **"Log it in seconds."** then **"See what's left."** | Music comes back in full. A crisp click on each tap, and a low thud when the number drops. |
| 20–26 | **The limit drops.** The phone screen pulls back into a simple bar labeled "Weekly limit." The bar shrinks in five steps, one per beat, with labels **90% → 80% → 70% → 60% → 50%**. A small "Missed?" tag pops up next to it and the bar keeps shrinking anyway. | Text: **"Every 4 weeks, it drops."** then **"Hit or miss."** | One heavy drum hit per step. |
| 26–30 | **The closing line.** The bar collapses into a single red line that underlines the closing text. The app name sits underneath. | Text: **"Half the spending."** Then the app name, **No Excuses**, lands big on the final hit. | The music ends on one hard final hit, then silence. |

## Tone
Fast. A tough-love coach, not a bank: quick cuts on the beat, short punchy text, no jokes and no cheesy moments. Same colors as the web page: off-white background, near-black text, tomato-red accent (#D7392B), Inter font.
